# How to Record Your First Sale in Perico

Perico gives you two ways to record a sale:

- **POS (Point of sale)** is a fast, barcode-first checkout for walk-in counter sales.
- **New sale** is a full order form for when you need to attach a customer, sell on credit, or add notes.

This guide covers both. Either way, Perico checks and deducts stock automatically and generates a printable invoice.

## Before you start

You'll need:

- An account that can reach the Sales area (Admin, Manager, or Staff)
- At least one **product** in your catalogue (add products under **Products**)
- At least one **warehouse** that holds stock

## Option A: Fast checkout with the POS

Best for quick, over-the-counter sales.

1. Open **POS** from the sidebar. You'll land on the *Point of sale* screen, with a scanner box on the left and your cart on the right.
2. Add products by scanning. Point a barcode scanner at the box that reads *"Point scanner here, or type a code + Enter,"* or type the barcode and press **Enter**. No scanner? Use the camera option to scan with your phone.
3. Each item drops into the **Cart**. Use the **+** and **−** buttons to change quantity, or remove a line you added by mistake.
4. Choose the **Warehouse** the stock should come from.
5. Add a **Discount (৳)** if you're giving one.
6. Check the **Total**, then click **Complete sale**.
7. Perico shows a **Sale complete** screen. Click **View invoice** to open, print, or share it.

Stock is deducted the moment the sale completes.

## Option B: Full invoice with New sale

Best when the sale needs a customer, credit terms, or notes.

1. Go to **Sales**, then click **New sale**.
2. Under **Line items**, pick a product **variant** and set the **Qty**. Click **Add line** to add more items.
3. In **Details**, choose the **Customer**. For a one-off buyer, leave it on **Walk-in / none**.
4. Set a **Discount (৳)** if there is one.
5. Under **Payment**, leave **"Paid in full now"** checked for a cash sale, or **uncheck it** to record the sale as credit. An unpaid sale goes straight to the customer's Dena-Paona (due) ledger.
6. Add **Notes** if you need to.
7. Check the **Total**, then click **Create sale**.

## What Perico does automatically

Completing a sale, either way, will:

- **Deduct stock** from the chosen warehouse and log the movement.
- **Record the order** and generate a printable invoice.
- **Add it to the customer's due ledger** (Dena-Paona) if it wasn't paid in full.
- **Update your reports**, so the sale shows up under Sales and on the Dashboard.

## What to try next

- **Returns:** open a sale under **Sales**, then use **Return** to send stock back and adjust the customer's balance.
- **Track dues:** see who owes what under the **Ledger (Dena-Paona)**.
- **Check the numbers:** find totals under **Reports**, including profit under **Reports → P&L**.
